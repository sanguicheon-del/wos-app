# Roadmap

## Current Focus
The project is evolving toward a polished product experience with a strong visual foundation, reusable design primitives, and clearly structured sections.

## Short-Term Priorities
- Continue refining the shared design system.
- Expand reusable components and section patterns.
- Strengthen accessibility and performance quality.
- Improve documentation and contributor guidance.

## Medium-Term Goals
- Extend the experience into additional product sections.
- Refine interaction patterns and motion language.
- Improve scalability of the component architecture.

## Long-Term Vision
Build a cohesive, premium digital product experience that is maintainable, accessible, and ready for growth.
