# Design System Prompt

## Goal
Contribute to or extend the W° OS design system in a consistent, reusable, and production-ready way.

## Scope
- Define or refine visual and interaction patterns.
- Ensure new UI patterns align with the existing design language.
- Promote reusability and consistency across the experience.

## Constraints
- Do not create disconnected or one-off patterns.
- Do not break existing design consistency.
- Keep the work scalable and maintainable.

## Deliverables
- A clear design system contribution or recommendation.
- Reusable patterns, tokens, or component guidance.
- Notes on consistency, accessibility, and implementation fit.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The contribution fits the existing design system.
- The pattern is reusable and understandable.
- The implementation is production-ready and coherent with the product experience.
