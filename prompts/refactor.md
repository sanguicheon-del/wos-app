# Refactor Prompt

## Goal
Refactor an existing implementation to improve clarity, maintainability, and long-term quality without breaking behavior.

## Scope
- Improve structure, readability, and organization.
- Reduce duplication and complexity where appropriate.
- Preserve current functionality while making the code easier to evolve.

## Constraints
- Do not introduce unnecessary churn.
- Do not change behavior unless required for correctness or maintainability.
- Keep the refactor scoped and reviewable.

## Deliverables
- A refactored implementation or structured improvement plan.
- Clear explanation of the rationale behind the changes.
- Notes on any risks or follow-up work.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The refactor improves clarity and maintainability.
- The implementation remains stable and production-safe.
- The changes are easy to review and understand.
