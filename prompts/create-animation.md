# Create Animation Prompt

## Goal
Create a polished animation for the W° OS experience that improves clarity, feel, and product quality.

## Scope
- Implement motion for transitions, reveals, interactions, or state changes.
- Align the animation with the project’s motion language and design philosophy.
- Ensure the result is smooth, lightweight, and accessible.

## Constraints
- Do not add unnecessary motion or visual noise.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep performance and maintainability in mind.
- Avoid introducing fragile or overly complex animation logic.

## Deliverables
- An animation implementation that fits the product style.
- Clear handling of timing, easing, and motion behavior.
- Notes on accessibility and performance considerations.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The animation feels intentional and premium.
- The motion is accessible and does not harm usability.
- The implementation is performant and easy to maintain.
