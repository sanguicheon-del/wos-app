# Landing Page Prompt

## Goal
Create or refine a high-quality landing page experience for W° OS that feels polished, premium, and conversion-focused.

## Scope
- Implement or improve landing page sections and composition.
- Align the experience with the project’s brand, design system, and product goals.
- Ensure the page is visually coherent, accessible, and performant.

## Constraints
- Do not introduce low-quality or distracting visual patterns.
- Keep the experience consistent with the project’s established style.
- Do not break existing layout or content structure.

## Deliverables
- A polished landing page implementation or improvement plan.
- Reusable section patterns where appropriate.
- Notes on accessibility, responsiveness, and quality.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The landing page feels premium and production-ready.
- The experience is accessible, coherent, and maintainable.
- The implementation aligns with the brand and design system.
