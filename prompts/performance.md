# Performance Prompt

## Goal
Identify and improve performance bottlenecks while preserving quality and user experience.

## Scope
- Review rendering, bundle cost, asset usage, and runtime efficiency.
- Recommend or implement improvements that reduce unnecessary overhead.
- Keep the product fast, smooth, and scalable.

## Constraints
- Do not degrade usability or visual quality.
- Do not introduce premature optimization or unnecessary complexity.
- Keep changes aligned with the project’s architecture and conventions.

## Deliverables
- A performance assessment or implementation plan.
- Concrete optimizations with rationale.
- Notes on tradeoffs and expected impact.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The solution improves efficiency without introducing regressions.
- The recommendations or implementation are relevant and practical.
- The result supports a high-quality production experience.
